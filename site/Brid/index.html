Overview

is a brid created by @crLms1n that was added on January 18, 2026.

Obtainment

Not even 10 studs away from you when you spawn. Just walk toward it and you get it.

Trivia

  • This Brid's hint reads 'At the start.' and its description reads 'Honestly, just a normal brid. In fact, so normal that it has won the National Normal award of Bridtopia for 6 straight years in a row. No tricks, no weird distractions, /e verynormal, none of that. Just a normal brid living a normal life, with no obstructions whatsoever. None.'
  • This Brid is one of the first 25 Brids added to the game, being the first Brid.
  • Before the precise rating system was removed, this Brid's rating was 1.00.
  • In this Brid's aforementioned description, the code for Upside-Down Brid is hidden.
