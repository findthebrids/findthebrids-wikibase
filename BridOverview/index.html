Overview

Brids are the main collectible in the game. As of this writing, there are 170 Brids in the game, ranging from trivially easy to unimaginably difficult. All Brids in the game are given a special name and design that is unique to them only.

Statistics

Main Stats

Each Brid is given five main statistics, those being Area, Description, Difficulty, Hint, and Series.

Area: The location in which a Brid is in. The area is not actually decided by where the Brid is at, but where the Brid starts, which in many cases overlap. However, in the case of Brids in sub-areas, this is necessary. Some Brids are in subrealms, in which case the Area is the name of the subrealm they are in.

Description: The description of the Brid. This is sometimes picked by the creator of the Brid, but in many cases the Brids are given descriptions to fit the Find the Brids lore.

Difficulty: The indication of how hard the Brid is to obtain. In order, the difficulties are Freebie, Easy, Medium, Hard, Challenging, Worrying, Extreme, Inhuman, Supreme, Impossible, Transcendant. The difficulties in the extreme-inhuman range are classified as 'mind-numbing', with them taking at least a decent amount of skill to obtain. Any Brid in the Transcendant difficulty is classified as 'brain-shattering' due to them needing extremely immense skill in puzzle solving/obbying.

Hint: A bit of information to help steer Brid hunters in the right direction when looking for the Brid.

Series: A set of Brids with a common idea are sometimes grouped into a Series. The only two series that exist as of writing this are Troll and Secret.

Substatistics

There are also four more statistics that are less important than the main ones, those being Badge ID, Credit, Is In Subrealm, and Required.

Badge ID: The ID of the badge that is to be awarded when a player obtains the specified Brid.

Credit: This is used internally to display who created the Brid in the Index.

Is In Subrealm: This is also used internally to display an area correctly is a Brid is in a subrealm.

Required: If the Brid is required, you must obtain it to complete the game. If not, the Brid is moved to a separate page within the Index and it is no longer required to complete the game.

Trivia

  • Brids are based off of a poorly drawn bird that the creator of the game drew on his homeroom teacher's whiteboard in his freshman year of high school.
